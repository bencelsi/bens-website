Murder of Haiku

Iambic roamed the streets with nought to do. ‘Twas dark, the sky was gray, the light was blue. And as Iambic walked the quiet street, He looked around for someone he could meet. “Hello!” he cried to some random Haiku. “Do I know you?” Haiku asked curiously. “I can’t quite recall.” “You don’t know me,” he said, “but I know you.” “How do you know me?” Haiku asked, getting worried. “I must be going...” “But that, I fear, I cannot let you do.” A Limerick was driving his car, When he heard a loud scream from afar. “Is somebody there?” Limerick cried through the air, “Hello? Please let me know where you are!” The body of the Haiku soon was found An old found poem found it on the ground. Iambic got away without a sound.
