Mr. Clinton, where to begin-tin... Your presidency was unprecedented. I do compliment it. Yes, you may have been pressed by the press, I confess, on your mistress, and all the mistrust that the whole matters mustered, and yes you were flustered. It’s all understood. Overall you did good. Except for one thing. In Rwanda, a bloodbath got warmer and warmer: Tutsis and Hutus were killing each other, well, mostly the latter the former. You had your informers, ‘bout all of the Hutu’s bad juju you knew, ooh, but what do you do? The Tutsis were not masochists, this was a massacre, one of the least classier. Was the cost to haul some helping hands to stop a holocaust in foreign lands much higher than the piles of bodies tossed to rot in empty lots? Y’all could have helped, instead you stalled, now all is lost, and all the tiny tots whose little lives were fraught with lots of blood and guts and violence, now are silenced. With razors and tasers, mace, who knows maybe laser guns, a race was laid waste to, houses were tastelessly raided, their babies misplaced, too. You may be Mr. Clinton, but you aren’t Mr. Bill. You don’t know pain to that extent, you probably never will. You toot C’s on your sax, why can’t you see the Tutsis screaming to you, killed as if behind your back? The next time you eat a Tootsie Roll, remember them, and with a frown, salute the Tutsis rolled into their graves because their skin was too light brown.
