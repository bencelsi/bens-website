A Letter to Jules, Y'Yen and Ben

(A message to Jules Renaud, Y’Yen Gallup, and Ben Van Raalte who are taking a motorcycle trip through Vietnam) To Jules Renaud, to Y’Yen and to Ben: I hope that I will see you guys again. But if I don’t, and this is sad to say, Most likely you have all three passed away. In this regard, I think that Vietnam Is basically a tickin’ frickin’ bomb. You could run out of money, gas or food, And be left stranded in the jungle, screwed. You could be ripped apart by vicious fangs, Or stabbed by rival motorcycle gangs. You might be hung, or starve, or even freeze You might contract a terrible disease. And if, to travel home, you buy a boat, You might find out too late it doesn’t float. You might somehow catch rabies from a fly, Or have to eat each other not to die. Or, worst of all, the dreaded death by chafe- Well anyway, good luck and come back safe.
