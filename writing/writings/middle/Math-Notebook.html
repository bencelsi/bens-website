Conversation with Math Notebook

Math Notebook: Hello, Ben. Me: Whoa… what? Why are you talking? Math Notebook: Why are you talking? Me: ??? Math Notebook: Well, Ben, I’ve had some things I wanted to ask you— Me: What is it? Math Notebook: What is the meaning of life, Ben? Me: How should I know? Math Notebook: Well, you are obviously some kind of God. All day you write in my pages, you seem to have some plan to your scribbles, and yet they are all random markings. You’ve created me, or at least what I am, with these meaningless writings. And, as you fill me with graphite, I know one day I will be full. Nowhere else to write, nothing left to do… it is the empty space which keeps me alive as much as the scribbles. One day you will use me up. Why will you do that, Ben? Why will you kill me? Me: It’s not my fault! It’s Mr. Avison and all his busywork! Math Notebook: It's your fault, Ben. You're the one turning me into a darkened shell of my former self. One day you'll throw me out like all the other dead math books. You're cruel. You're a murderer. You're insane. Me: What? I'm insane? How? Math Notebook: You're talking to a math notebook. Me: Oh. Right. THE END
