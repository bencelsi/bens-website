Trick or Treat Origins

Once upon a time there was an evil witch named Evil Witch (first name Evil, last name Witch.) One day Evil Witch went to the village on her everyday hunt for babies. She would walk up to someone's house, ring the doorbell, and ask the occupant if they had a baby in their possession. If they did, she would ask to see it. Every self-respecting person would go fetch the baby, bring it to her and hold it up for her to see. At this point Evil Witch would ask to hold the baby, and when the person said yes, she would take the baby and eat it. This went on for some time until people become aware of her evil intentions, and instead of giving her a baby, they would give her something that tasted about the same as a baby, such as a Tootsie Roll. Evil Witch ate the Toorsie Roll and left the people their babies. Eventually, people who wanted to eat Tootsie Rolls (or occasionally babies) dressed up as Evil Wirch, and went around demanding a baby or a treat. Over time this somehow turned to "Trick or Treat."
